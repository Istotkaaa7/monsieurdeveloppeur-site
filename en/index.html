Frequently asked questions

You might be wondering…

Here are the answers to the questions our clients ask most. If yours isn’t here, just get in touch.

How long until my website is ready?

Between 2 and 12 weeks depending on the size of the project. A simple website sits at the lower end. The exact timeline is stated in the quote and also depends on when we receive your photos and texts.

I don’t have texts or good photos. Is that a problem?

No. We help you write the texts and choose your best photos. Just mention it in the form.

Can I edit my website myself?

Yes. We show you how to change a price, a photo or a text. And if you prefer, we can take care of it for you.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes, we also do redesigns. Put your website address in the form — we’ll look at it before we reply.

Can the website be in several languages?

Yes. The Tourism package includes French and English. Other languages are available as options.

You’re in Madagascar. How do we work together?

Fully remotely: video calls, WhatsApp and e-mail. That’s how we work with clients in Réunion, Mauritius, France, Switzerland and Poland.
